This notice explains the rights you have over your personal data under the GDPR and how to exercise them with {{legal_name}} (“{{brand_name}}”), the data controller. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

Your rights

  • Access (Art. 15) — obtain confirmation and a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) — request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) — limit processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability (Art. 20) — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it transmitted to another controller where technically feasible.
  • Objection (Art. 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling and direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) — withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Automated decisions (Art. 22) — request human review of decisions made solely by automated means that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

How to exercise them

Email {{email_privacy}} or our Data Protection Officer at {{email_dpo}}. Access and portability requests can also be served in-app via the data-export function. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Our response time

We respond within one month of receiving a valid request. This period may be extended by two further months for complex or numerous requests; we will tell you if so. Requests are handled free of charge unless manifestly unfounded or excessive.

Automated processing

Some eligibility and safety checks (for example driver onboarding decisions) involve automated evaluation. You can request human review, express your point of view and contest the outcome by contacting us at the addresses above.
