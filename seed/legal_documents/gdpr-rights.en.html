1. How to make a request

  • In app: Settings → Privacy → My data → choose the right to exercise.
  • By email: {{email_privacy}} with your name, account email and the right requested.
  • DPO: for sensitive requests or internal complaints, {{email_dpo}}.

We reply within 30 days (Art. 12 GDPR), extendable by 60 days for complex or numerous requests, with a reasoned notice.

2. Right of access (Art. 15)

We confirm whether we process your data and give you a copy of what we hold: identifying data, rides, ratings, uploaded documents (for drivers), consent logs, support tickets, payment data (references, not card details). You receive an export in JSON or CSV via a secure link.

3. Right to rectification (Art. 16)

You can edit profile data in app. For data you cannot edit yourself (e.g. date of birth, onboarding documents) write to {{email_privacy}}: we update within 7 working days.

4. Right to erasure (Art. 17)

You can request account deletion. Procedure:

  1. Open Settings → Privacy → Delete account.
  2. You receive an email with a signed URL valid for 7 days to confirm.
  3. Upon confirmation, we anonymise your identifying data (name, email, phone).

Honest limit. Some data stays for legal obligation (ride metadata and billing for 10 years, DAC7 data for the statutory period). Such data remains linked to an anonymous id, not to your name.

5. Right to restriction (Art. 18)

You can ask us to suspend processing pending a verification (e.g. accuracy dispute, ongoing objection). During restriction the data remains stored but not processed, except to defend a right in court.

6. Right to portability (Art. 20)

You receive your data in structured, machine-readable format (JSON). You can also ask us to transmit it directly to another controller, where technically feasible. The in-app export produces a downloadable archive in 7 days.

7. Right to object (Art. 21)

You may object to processing based on our legitimate interest (security, anti-fraud, non-personalised analytics). We will examine the request and, unless compelling legitimate grounds exist, suspend processing. Objection to direct marketing is always granted.

8. Automated decisions (Art. 22)

You have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing producing legal or significant effects. On {{brand_name}} this means:

  • For every decision concerning you (matching, price, suspension), you can request human intervention.
  • Driver suspension is always confirmed by a human operator before becoming final.
  • To contest a decision, write to {{email_privacy}} explaining your case; we reply within 14 days.

Operational detail in DPIA Summary — Automated Decisions.
