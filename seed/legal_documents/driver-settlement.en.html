1. Pricing architecture

When a request comes in, price-arbiter computes the price in the following canonical order:

base       = base_fare + (distance_m/1000)*per_km + (duration_s/60)*per_min   ← Cadastre zone
× driver modifier            (inside zone band, additive with surge)
× time modifier              (night / weekend, if configured)
× surge                      (capped at zone ceiling, typically 2.0×)
− discounts                   (platform-funded coupons, passenger side)
= subtotal
+ fees[]                     (platform commission, any authority levies)
+ taxes[]                    (VAT per zone tax profile)
= total (passenger amount)

The platform commission defaults to {{platform_commission_percent}}% on the subtotal (default {{platform_commission_default}}). Can be overridden per zone.

2. Worked example

Ride Bolzano centre → Bolzano Sud, distance 5 km, duration 12 min, zone base 3.00€, per_km 1.80, per_min 0.30, fee 12.5%.

base          = 3.00 + 5*1.80 + 12*0.30  = 3.00 + 9.00 + 3.60   = 15.60
surge x1.0    (no surge)                                           = 15.60
modifier +5% (additive)                15.60 + 0.78               = 16.38
discounts     = 0
subtotal      = 16.38
fee 12.5%     = 16.38 * 0.125                                      = 2.05
passenger total                                                    = 18.43

Stripe Connect:
- passenger charge                                                 = 18.43
- destination amount to driver   (total - fee)                     = 16.38
- platform retains (application_fee)                               = 2.05

If the zone has an active VAT profile (e.g. it-driver-supplier), a VAT line appears in taxes[] and lowers the driver net accordingly. If the zone is it-deemed-supplier (ViDA), VAT is on top and the passenger pays it separately; the platform remits it.

3. Price band and driver modifier

For each Cadastre zone we define modifier_min_multiplier and modifier_max_multiplier: your modifier choice (e.g. +10% / −5%) must fall inside the band. The band is tuned to local NCC tariff references so the platform stays inside the marketplace intermediation perimeter (CJEU C-434/15). Surge and modifier are additive on the subtotal, not multiplicative: a +20% surge combined with a +10% modifier produces +30% on base, not +32%.

4. Stripe Connect — cash flow

We use Stripe Connect with destination charge: the passenger payment lands on the platform Stripe account and Stripe automatically transfers (application_fee) to your connected Stripe account net of the platform commission. No manual steps: settlement is atomic at capture.

To enable payouts, complete Stripe Connect onboarding from your profile. Stripe requires KYC info (tax code, VAT, IBAN); your verification level is visible in app as payouts_enabled.

5. Weekly payout

The payout job runs every Monday at 03:00 UTC and processes successfully captured rides up to the previous Sunday. The transfer typically arrives in 1-3 business days. Minimum threshold and cadence are configurable via the operator panel. If your Stripe account is suspended or unverified, the payout goes on hold (held state) until resolved.

6. Cancellations and no-show

The cancellation policy is per zone and per stage:

  • after_grace: passenger cancels after the grace window but before pickup. Platform retains a fee; you receive a compensation (flat or percentage, per-zone).
  • in_progress: cancellation during the ride via mutual handshake (full_refund, full_pay, partial:NN). Platform fee is per zone; the split between you and the passenger comes from the handshake outcome.
  • no_show: driver reports passenger absence after the wait timer. No_show fee captured from the passenger's payment method.

Current percentages for your zone are visible in app before accepting the ride and in the Cadastre panel.

7. Set-off, holdback, chargeback

We may withhold or recover from credits amounts owed by you (passenger refunds, reversals, chargebacks, penalties, improperly received amounts) within legal limits. We notify by email each withholding with reasons. You have the right to contest within 14 days.

8. DAC7 and invoicing

We report annually to the Italian Tax Agency your data (tax code, VAT, residence), gross consideration, retained commission and number of operations, under D.Lgs. 32/2023 (DAC7). You receive a copy of the data reported under your name.

The ride invoice to the passenger is your responsibility (you are the carrier). The platform issues its own invoice for the intermediation commission, settled by offset on your net.
