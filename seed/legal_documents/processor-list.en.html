1. What this register is

When we entrust an external supplier with processing personal data on our behalf (e.g. SMS delivery, payment processing, geocoding, monitoring), that supplier is a data processor under Art. 28 GDPR. For each one we indicate role, location, transfer mechanism (if extra-EEA) and DPA status.

3. Sub-processors

Main processors in turn rely on sub-processors (e.g. cloud hosting, CDN, monitoring). Active sub-processors are listed in the public registers of the respective suppliers (Stripe, Twilio, Google Cloud) or, on request, communicated at {{email_privacy}}. Any new sub-processor of sensitive suppliers (payments, identity, communications) is notified to us and reflected here within 30 days.

4. Extra-EEA transfers

Transfers to third countries without adequacy decisions rely on the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses 2021/914 (SCCs), supplemented by technical supplementary measures where needed (at-rest encryption, tokenisation, contractual transparency reporting, authority-request standards). For the United States we consider the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the supplier adheres.

5. Change notifications

We notify the addition or change of main processors with reasonable advance notice (at least 30 days), via email to drivers and in-app. You have the right to object by terminating the account; otherwise continued use constitutes acceptance.
