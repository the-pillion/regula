1. Self-employed status

You operate as an independent self-employed worker. Nothing in these terms creates a relationship of subordinate employment, agency, association or joint venture between you and {{brand_name}}. There is no exclusivity: you may work via other platforms, accept or refuse any request, choose your hours and zones. {{brand_name}} does not direct how you perform the transport, beyond what is required for safety and compliance, and does not guarantee request volume, minimum earnings or continuity of work.

2. Requirements and continuous compliance

You must hold and keep valid:

  • all licences, authorisations and registrations required by law for passenger transport in the zone where you operate;
  • a third-party RCA insurance policy suitable for passenger transport ("uso terzi");
  • a vehicle homologated for commercial use, maintained in working order and serviced;
  • the onboarding, document and eligibility checks {{brand_name}} requires. We may re-verify at any time.

You must promptly notify us of any expiry, suspension or invalidity of any licence, authorisation, insurance or document. The platform blocks new ride acceptance at the first expired document.

3. Italy — NCC framework

If you operate in Italy you fall under the NCC (Noleggio Con Conducente) regime governed by Law No. 21 of 15 January 1992 and regional/municipal implementing rules. Non-negotiable minimum requirements:

  • Municipal NCC authorisation issued by the comune of the garage (doc autorizzazione_ncc).
  • Garage declaration in good standing; the garage must sit in the same comune as the authorisation (doc rimessa_declaration; see also the Bersani return-to-base rule).
  • Drivers Register entry at the Chamber of Commerce (doc ruolo_conducenti).
  • CAP KB — professional driving certificate (doc cap_kb).
  • Driving licence with minimum 3-year seniority (Patente A for moto, Patente B for car).
  • Vehicle registered for "uso terzi" under art. 82 Italian Highway Code (registration with the proper annotation).
  • RCA insurance policy extended to passenger-with-driver transport for third-party use.
  • Active VAT for transport activity.

More detail (procedure, documents, checks) in the NCC Italy specific schedule.

6. Commission, settlement, payments

{{brand_name}} applies a platform commission on each completed ride, configurable per zone, disclosed at onboarding and in app before you accept each ride. The commission is the price of the intermediation service and is invoiced.

Fares are collected on your behalf via Stripe Connect (destination charge) and credited to your linked account. We may offset, hold back or recover from credits refunds, chargebacks, cancellation reversals, penalties or improperly received amounts, within legal limits. Numerical detail (worked example, payout calendar, fee/tax/ levy stack) in the Settlement & Payment Disclosure.

7. Price band and driver modifier

You have tariff autonomy within a min/max band set per zone (Cadastre modifier_min_multiplier / modifier_max_multiplier). Your modifier (e.g. +10% / −5%) is additive, not multiplicative, on the subtotal: surge and modifier sum on the subtotal, they do not compose. Surge is capped by the zone ceiling (typically 2.0×) and can never lower the price below your modifier (lower-bound at arbiter level).

Honest limit. The band is set per zone by the platform operator based on local NCC tariff references. Even though you choose the value inside the band, the band itself is set by us. This is a factor considered by case-law on marketplace vs carrier qualification (CJEU C-434/15) and is a deliberate design choice.

8. Cancellation, no-show, mutual handshake

Per-zone cancellation policies define the platform fee and your compensation for each of three stages: after_grace (passenger cancels before pickup), in_progress (cancellation during ride, handled via mutual handshake — full_refund, full_pay, partial:NN), no_show (passenger absent at pickup). All shares are published in your zone's Cadastre section and visible in app.

10. EU Platform Work Directive

Directive (EU) 2024/2831 (Platform Work Directive) enters into force with transposition by 2 December 2026. It introduces a legal presumption of subordinate employment when certain control indicators are present. {{brand_name}} has designed the platform to not exercise such indicators: no imposed hours, no exclusivity, no disciplinary sanctions beyond safety and compliance, ratings as transparency not punishment. Final qualification is determined by the law at transposition and by the competent authority.
