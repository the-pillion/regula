4. Pricing and Payment

4.1 Fare Structure

Pillion displays a fare estimate based on:

  • Base rate for the operating zone (set by Pillion)
  • Driver-applied pricing modifier (±50% of the base rate, at the Driver's discretion)
  • Real-time route calculation

The final fare may differ slightly from the estimate due to route deviations, traffic, or tolls. Passengers are shown the estimate before confirming a booking and will be charged the actual completed fare.

4.2 Platform Fee

Pillion charges Drivers a platform service fee of 12.5% of each completed ride fare. This fee covers payment processing, compliance infrastructure, insurance facilitation, and platform operations. The fee is deducted from the Driver's payout automatically.

4.3 Payment Processing
