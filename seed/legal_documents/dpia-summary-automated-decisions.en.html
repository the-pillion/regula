DPIA Summary — Automated Decisions

Version {{version}} · Last updated: {{last_updated}} · Effective: {{effective_date}}

At a glance

  • Four platform decisions are partially automated: matching, offer-pool ranking, eligibility filters, safety blocks.
  • Price calculation uses declared deterministic rules (zone band + surge cap + driver modifier), not an opaque ML model.
  • Suspension is always confirmed by a human operator before becoming final.
  • You have the right to obtain human intervention, express your view and contest the decision (GDPR Art. 22).
  • The full DPIA file is available to supervisory authorities on request.

1. Why a DPIA

GDPR Art. 22 limits decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or significant effects. For drivers, an automatic suspension would have significant effects on work; for passengers, a matching decision may affect access to the service. This DPIA documents design choices preventing a disproportionate effect.

3. What is not automated

  • Initial driver approval: a human operator verifies documents, NCC, garage, "uso terzi", RCA.
  • Final suspension: the automation may temporarily block; suspension lasting beyond the review window is confirmed by an operator.
  • Reinstatement: always human.
  • Cancellation and no-show dispute resolution: human.
  • Safety-report decisions: the PrefactGuard flow includes human review.
  • Per-zone price band: set by a human operator based on local NCC tariff references, not by an algorithm.

4. Risks and mitigations

Indirect discrimination in rankingWeights published to driver in app; periodic audit of per-zone metrics
Erroneous suspensionMandatory human confirmation, 14-day contestation window

Compliance biasRules are deterministic and declared; same configuration applies to every driver in the zone

5. Right to human intervention (GDPR Art. 22)

For every automated decision concerning you, you can:

  1. Ask for human intervention by writing to {{email_privacy}}.
  2. Express your view and provide additional information.
  3. Contest the decision and request review.

We reply within 14 days. During the review, any automatic blocks are suspended unless an ascertained safety risk applies.

6. Transparency on inputs

Through your right of access (Art. 15) you can obtain a copy of the inputs that produced a decision concerning you: online presence at the time, compliance outcome, any relevant PrefactGuard signals, offer data used for matching. We do not share the internal weights of fine-grained models for anti-fraud reasons, but we provide a comprehensible explanation of the logic.
