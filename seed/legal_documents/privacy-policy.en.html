2. What data we collect

2.1 Account data

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • Authentication credentials (managed by our identity provider; passwords are never stored in plaintext)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Date of birth (required for driver eligibility and age verification)
  • Preferred language and locale

2.2 Driver onboarding data

  • Government-issued identity documents (ID card, passport, driving licence)
  • Vehicle registration and insurance certificates
  • Professional transport licences where required by local law
  • Background and eligibility check outcomes (decision summary, not the full underlying file)
  • Payout bank details (held by our payment processor, not on our servers)

2.3 Ride and location data

  • Pickup and drop-off locations and geocoded addresses
  • GPS route coordinates during an active trip
  • Trip timestamps, duration, distance, fare and status
  • One-time pickup verification codes (discarded after use)

2.4 Payment data

Payments are handled by our payment processor. We store only a customer reference, payment-intent reference and transaction status. Full card numbers and bank credentials are never stored on our infrastructure.

2.5 Communications and technical data

  • In-app messages, support tickets and email correspondence
  • Push notification tokens and SMS verification codes (codes not retained after delivery)
  • IP address, device model, operating system, app version and session identifiers
  • Error logs, crash reports and cookie identifiers (see our Cookie Policy)

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures including encryption in transit and at rest, strong password hashing, role-based access control, audit logging, least-privilege service access and scheduled purging of location data.
