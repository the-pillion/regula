2. What Data We Collect

We collect personal data depending on how you interact with our platform:

2.1 Account Registration

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • Password (stored as a bcrypt hash — never in plaintext)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Date of birth (required for driver compliance checks)
  • Preferred language and locale

2.2 Driver Onboarding

  • Government-issued identity documents (passport, national ID, driving licence)
  • Vehicle registration and insurance certificates
  • Professional licences (VTC, P-Schein, NCC or equivalent per jurisdiction)
  • Background check results (decision summary only — not full PII payload)
  • Bank account details for payouts (processed by Stripe Connect — not stored on our servers)

2.3 Ride Data

  • Pickup and drop-off locations
  • GPS route coordinates during active rides
  • Ride timestamps, duration, and distance
  • Fare amount and pricing modifier applied
  • Ride status and lifecycle events
  • OTP codes (one-time, immediately discarded after use)

2.4 Location Data

  • Driver GPS position while the app is active (used for matching and ETA)
  • Geocoded pickup/drop-off addresses (processed via fastgeo.net, our in-house geocoding service)
  • Historical GPS data from completed rides is anonymised (coordinates nullified) after 90 days

2.5 Payment Data

All payment transactions are processed by Stripe (Stripe Technology Europe, Ltd.). Pillion stores only a Stripe customer ID, payment intent reference, and transaction status. Full card details, PAN, and bank credentials are never stored on our infrastructure.

2.6 Communications

  • In-app messages and support tickets
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase / OneSignal)
  • SMS verification codes (Twilio/Vonage — not stored after delivery)
  • Email correspondence

2.7 Technical Data

  • IP address, browser type, device model and OS
  • App version and session identifiers
  • Error logs and crash reports
  • Cookie identifiers (see our Cookie Policy)

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the marketplace and match drivers with passengers
  • To verify driver eligibility and document compliance per local regulations
  • To process payments and transfer earnings to drivers via Stripe Connect
  • To provide real-time routing, ETA calculations, and fare estimates
  • To send transactional communications (booking confirmations, receipts, OTP codes)
  • To handle safety incidents, SOS alerts, and emergency contacts
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • To comply with tax reporting requirements (Italy: D.Lgs. 241/97; EU DAC7)
  • To improve our platform through aggregated, anonymised analytics
  • To send marketing updates where you have given explicit consent

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe — Payment processing and driver payouts (EU data residency)
  • TomTom — Routing and map tile requests (anonymised coordinates)
  • fastgeo.net — Geocoding of addresses (our in-house service, EU-hosted)
  • Checkr — Background checks for driver onboarding (US company, SCCs apply)
  • Twilio / Vonage — SMS delivery for OTP and notifications
  • Firebase (Google) — Push notification delivery
  • OneSignal — Push notification management
  • Postmark — Transactional email delivery
  • Competent authorities — Where required by law (law enforcement, tax authorities, regulators)

All third-party processors are bound by Data Processing Agreements (DPA) compliant with GDPR Art. 28. For transfers outside the EEA, appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions) are in place.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject in the EU/EEA, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure / "Right to be forgotten" (Art. 17) — Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18) — Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format. Use GET /api/user/export-data in the Pillion app.
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) — Withdraw marketing consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Right not to be subject to automated decisions (Art. 22) — Request human review of automated compliance decisions

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@pillion.eu. We respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

8. Security Measures

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures including:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all API and web communications
  • AES-256 encryption at rest for sensitive data
  • Bcrypt password hashing (cost factor 12+)
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) for internal systems
  • Regular dependency audits and penetration testing
  • Automated GPS data purging after 90 days
  • GDPR-compliant anonymisation on account deletion

In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours and affected users without undue delay, as required by GDPR Art. 33–34.
