2. What we collect

2.1 Account data

  • Name, email, phone, profile photo (optional), language
  • Date of birth (age check and driver eligibility)
  • Credentials managed by Zitadel (passwords never stored in clear)

2.2 Driver onboarding data

  • ID documents and driving licences
  • NCC authorisation, drivers register, CAP KB, garage declaration
  • Vehicle registration (with "uso terzi" annotation), RCA insurance
  • Tax code, VAT, bank data (held by the payment processor)
  • Verification outcomes (decision summary, not the full file)

2.3 Ride and geolocation data

  • Pickup/dropoff points, geocoded addresses
  • GPS traces during the active ride
  • Times, duration, distance, fare, ride status
  • Pickup PIN (see §9)

2.4 Payment data

Payments are handled by the payment service provider (Stripe). We keep only a customer reference, the operation reference and the transaction status. Full card numbers and bank credentials are never on our servers.

2.5 Communications and technical data

  • In-app messages, support tickets, email
  • Push notification tokens, SMS codes (codes not stored after sending)
  • IP, device model, OS, app version, session identifiers
  • Error logs, crash reports, cookie identifiers (see Cookie Policy)

5. Service architecture (who processes what)

The {{brand_name}} platform is composed of specialised services, each with a defined purpose:

  • pillion-core — identity, onboarding, compliance, payments, audit. Source of truth on people.
  • ride-matcher — ride matching and orchestration. No PII; uses nullable external ids.
  • price-arbiter — price, fee, tax computation. No PII.
  • cadastre — zone geometry and per-city rate cards.
  • compass / gps-ingest — GPS collection and streaming.
  • prefactguard — trust & safety, background check, risk signals.
  • regula — legal documents, acceptances, consents, processor register.
  • herald — notification delivery (push, SMS, email).
  • fastgeo — routing and geocoding.

This technical separation matches a separation of purposes: each service handles only the data it needs. The Processor Register lists the external suppliers attached to each service.

6. Recipients, processors, extra-EEA transfers

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with processors acting on our instructions (payments, maps and routing, SMS and push notifications, hosting, monitoring) and with competent authorities where required by law. The full list is in the Processor and Sub-Processor Register. All processors are bound by Art. 28 GDPR agreements. Extra-EEA transfers rely on adequacy decisions or EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

7. Automated decisions (GDPR Art. 22)

Some platform functions involve automated decisions:

  • Driver matching and ranking in the offer pool;
  • Price calculation (band + surge + driver modifier);
  • Eligibility filters (expired documents, insurance coverage, allowed zone);
  • Safety blocks on the first critical signal.

Driver suspension is always confirmed by a human operator before becoming final. You have the right to obtain human intervention, express your view, contest the decision by writing to {{email_privacy}}. Reply within one month (Art. 12). Details in DPIA Summary — Automated Decisions.

8. DAC7 reporting

Under Directive (EU) 2021/514 transposed by D.Lgs. 32/2023, we report annually to the Italian Tax Agency (Agenzia delle Entrate) driver identifying data (name, tax code, VAT, date of birth, residence), gross consideration earned, number of operations and commissions retained. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Reporting is performed in XML through official AdE channels.

9. Pickup PIN

When the driver arrives at pickup, we generate a 6-digit PIN with a cryptographically secure RNG (CSPRNG). The PIN is visible to the passenger for 5 minutes and accepts a maximum of 3 verification attempts. On the server we keep only the salted SHA-256 hash of the PIN; the plaintext is never persisted. After use or window expiry, only the hash survives for audit up to 30 days. Purpose: prevent person swaps and fraudulent pickups.

11. Security

We adopt appropriate technical and organisational measures: TLS 1.2+ in transit and encryption at rest, robust password hashing through Zitadel, role-based access control (RBAC), audit logs, least privilege, network isolation of sensitive services, scheduled deletion of GPS data. In case of a breach posing risk to your rights, we notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours (Art. 33) and you without undue delay (Art. 34).

12. Minors

The platform is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data of minors and we delete it if we become aware.
