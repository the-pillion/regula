1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device, remember your preferences, and provide a better user experience. Cookies cannot execute code or deliver viruses, and they do not access other files on your device.

Similar technologies include localStorage (used by our cookie consent manager to store your preference), sessionStorage, and pixel tags used by analytics services.

3. Third-Party Services and Their Cookies

Some functionality on our site is provided by third parties who may set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts — Fonts are loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log your IP address. We are evaluating self-hosting fonts to eliminate this dependency.
  • Phosphor Icons — Icons are loaded from unpkg CDN. No tracking cookies are set.
  • Stripe.js — Used on checkout pages for PCI-compliant payment forms. Stripe may set cookies for fraud prevention purposes. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

4. How to Manage Your Cookie Preferences

When you first visit our site, a cookie consent banner allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for the site to operate.

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. We honour DNT signals by not setting analytics or marketing cookies when a DNT signal is detected.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our practices or applicable law changes. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent revision. Significant changes will be communicated via the cookie consent banner on your next visit.
