1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files the site or app stores on your device. We also use similar technologies such as web storage, pixels, third-party SDKs. In this policy "cookie" covers these technologies too. Collection follows Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code (D.Lgs. 196/2003), and the Garante guidelines of 10 June 2021.

2. Categories and purposes

We split cookies into four categories:

CategoryConsentPurpose
Strictly necessaryNot required (art. 122(2))Authentication, security, load balancing, storage of consent preferences. The service does not work without them.
FunctionalRequiredStore language, last zone, UI preferences. Improve the experience but are not necessary.
AnalyticsRequiredMeasure use of the site/app in aggregated form. May include third-party tools.
MarketingRequiredPersonalised content and campaign effectiveness measurement. May involve profiling.

4. Consent management

On first access you see a banner with three equally prominent options: Accept all, Reject all, Customise. Scrolling, closing the banner with the "X" or staying on the site do not constitute consent and leave you in the default state (only strictly necessary cookies active).

You can change or withdraw consent at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer or in app settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing already performed.

5. Extra-EEA transfers

Some suppliers (especially analytics and marketing) are based in the United States or other non-EEA countries. Transfers rely on EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) 2021/914 and case-by-case supplementary measures. Detail in the Processor Register.

6. Consent retention

We keep proof of consent (date, time, category choice, policy version, cookie banner identifier) for 12 months from collection or withdrawal, for accountability (Art. 7(1) GDPR).
