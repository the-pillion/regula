1. Regulatory framework

NCC activity in Italy is governed by:

  • Law No. 21 of 15 January 1992 — regulation of non-scheduled public passenger transport (taxi and NCC).
  • D.L. 4 July 2006 No. 223 (Bersani decree), converted into L. 248/2006 — return-to-garage rule.
  • D.M. 215/1991 — requirements for drivers register entry.
  • D.Lgs. 285/1992 (Italian Highway Code), in particular art. 82 on "uso proprio" vs "uso terzi" homologation.
  • Regional and municipal implementing rules, which may add requirements (minimum tariffs, liveries, badges).

3. Return-to-garage rule

The Bersani decree requires that, between rides, the NCC car return (or remain in the province) of the declared garage. The platform does not impose return tracking, but assumes you respect the rule; reports of violation may lead to suspension and to communications to the competent authority. The scope and interpretation of the rule have been subject to legislative and judicial interventions; check your area's municipal resolutions.

4. "Uso terzi" vehicle

Art. 82 of the Italian Highway Code distinguishes between "uso proprio" (private transport) and "uso terzi" (paid third-party transport) homologation. For NCC the vehicle must be registered or re-homologated for "uso terzi", with the proper annotation on the registration book. The platform refuses onboarding of "uso proprio" vehicles: verification happens at registration approval via the uso_terzi checkbox and manual review of the image.

5. What we verify

  • Presence and legibility of all documents listed in §2.
  • Consistency between NCC authorisation comune and garage comune (automatic cross-check at garage document approval).
  • "Uso terzi" annotation on the vehicle registration (manual review).
  • Minimum 3-year licence seniority.
  • Expiries: the system blocks new ride acceptance at the first expired document.

6. What you self-certify

  • Actual ownership of the NCC authorisation and garage as declared.
  • Renewals at the comune and Chamber of Commerce kept up to date.
  • Compliance with municipal and regional resolutions (liveries, badges, minimum tariffs).
  • Compliance with the return-to-garage rule.
  • Vehicle maintenance and roadworthiness inspection (revisione) validity.

7. Expiries and renewals

We notify upcoming expiries by email and in app (30 days and 7 days before). The expiry notice is a courtesy: renewal is your responsibility. Expired documents block new ride acceptance until renewed.

8. Checks and suspension

During a check by Polizia Locale, Carabinieri or Guardia di Finanza, you must show original documents. The platform cooperates with authorities on motivated requests. Substantiated reports of violation (non-matching garage, "uso proprio", revoked authorisation) may lead to precautionary suspension; suspension is always confirmed by a human operator and can be contested (see Driver TOS §14).
