At a glance

  • We track driver position only when online, and vehicle position only during an active ride. Never 24/7.
  • Precision is what matching and safety need: no behavioural fingerprint.
  • Ride GPS traces are deleted within 30 days; only aggregated metadata stays for audit and internal analytics.
  • You can limit tracking by going offline (driver) or ending the ride (passenger).

1. Why a DPIA

Systematic location monitoring is one of the activities for which the Italian Garante (decision no. 467 of 11 October 2018) and the EDPB consider an Art. 35 GDPR impact assessment necessary. This summary describes our design choices and the limits we set.

2. What we collect

  • Online-driver position: latitude, longitude, accuracy, timestamp.
  • Vehicle position during an active ride: same structure, adaptive frequency (higher in pickup phase, lower on smooth tracks).
  • Passenger position at request time: used for matching and then discarded.

We do not collect position outside these windows. We do not build historical mobility profiles on individuals. We do not sell location data to third parties.

3. When we collect it

  • Driver: while you are online (in dispatch). Going offline stops the stream.
  • Passenger: only at ride request time (for matching) and during the active ride (for tracking).
  • SOS: triggered on explicit request; shared with emergency contacts and operator.

4. Risks and mitigations

RiskMitigation
Unauthorised surveillance of driver off-hoursStreaming only when online; immediate stop on offline.
Behavioural profiling of usersNo ML on individual mobility patterns; aggregated metrics only.
Unauthorised access to GPS tracesAt-rest encryption, RBAC, audit logs, access only by security operators.
Excessive retentionAutomatic deletion of traces at 30 days (privacy:purge-old-locations job).
Cleartext transmissionTLS 1.2+ mandatory on client ↔ gps-ingest ↔ compass paths.
Disclosure to third partiesOnly to explicitly enabled emergency contacts or authorities by law.

5. Retention

Ride GPS traces are deleted within 30 days. Ride metadata (distance, duration, fare) is kept for tax obligations (10 years) but does not include the route geometry, only geocoded pickup and dropoff points. Deletion is automatic and tracked.

6. Data subject rights

For exercising rights (access, erasure, objection) see Your GDPR Rights. To object to geolocation during a ride, you must end it. To disable tracking as a driver, go offline. The platform cannot operate without active geolocation during an active ride.
