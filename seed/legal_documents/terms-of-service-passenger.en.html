1. What this contract is

When you book a ride, you enter into two distinct contracts: (a) the transport contract directly with the independent NCC driver who accepts the request; (b) the intermediation contract with {{legal_name}} for use of the {{brand_name}} platform, governed by these terms. {{brand_name}} is not a carrier and is not a party to the transport contract.

2. Account and eligibility

You must be at least 18 and hold an account authenticated via Zitadel (our identity provider). You must provide truthful information and protect your credentials. You may book a ride for another person; in that case you remain responsible for performance toward the driver.

3. Booking and price

Before booking you see a quote computed by price-arbiter from the zone rate card (Cadastre): base fare, distance, time, any surge and driver-modifier surcharges. Surge surcharges are capped by a zone ceiling (typically 2.0×); the driver modifier moves within a per-zone min/max band. Surge and modifier are additive on the subtotal, not multiplicative.

Confirming a ride authorises a pre-authorisation on your payment method for the maximum quoted amount. At the end, the actual amount is captured and any excess released. The platform refuses rides beyond a maximum per-zone distance and verifies the fare server-side (signed quote_id).

4. Pickup PIN

When the driver arrives at the pickup point, you receive in-app a 6-digit PIN generated server-side (CSPRNG, 5-minute TTL, max 3 attempts). You must share the PIN with the driver before departure. Without verified PIN, the ride does not transition to "in progress". The PIN protects against person swaps and fraudulent pickups.

Honest limit. The PIN is stored server-side as a salted SHA-256 hash; the plaintext value is visible to you for the window (5 minutes) and then only the hash survives for audit.

5. Cancellation, no-show, mutual handshake

You can cancel for free inside the grace window (zone-configurable, typically 2 minutes). Beyond that, the zone cancellation policy applies, split into three stages:

  • after_grace — passenger cancels before pickup: platform fee + driver compensation, per zone percentages.
  • in_progress — cancellation during the ride: mutual handshake with three outcomes: full_refund, full_pay, partial:NN (a partial share between 1 and 99%). The platform retains its fee per zone policy.
  • no_show — the driver reports your absence at pickup after the wait timer expires: zone no_show fee applies.

Per-stage percentages and min/max caps are published in your zone's Cadastre section and visible in app when the fee is computed. A cancellation dispute is resolved via {{email_support}}; the final decision is taken by a human operator.

6. Payment and receipt

Payment is processed via Stripe Connect with destination charge: the passenger amount lands on the driver's account, net of the platform fee and any tax/levy. You receive a receipt by email and in-app. The driver, where applicable, issues their own NCC invoice/receipt under Italian law. {{brand_name}} is not the carrier and does not issue the transport invoice; we issue our own invoice for the platform fee where applicable.

7. Ratings and reports

You can rate a ride along multiple dimensions (hygiene, safety, driving quality). Ratings are a transparency tool, not automatic sanctions: they do not cause deactivation without human intervention. A serious safety report triggers the PrefactGuard flow and is always reviewed by an operator.

8. Safety and SOS

The in-app SOS feature shares your position with the emergency contacts you configured and with our team. In an emergency, always call 112 (European single emergency number) first. Pillion does not replace emergency services.

9. Automated decisions (GDPR Art. 22)

Driver matching, ranking, price calculation (band + surge) and safety blocks are partially automated. You have the right to request human intervention, express your view and contest the decision by writing to {{email_privacy}}. Details in our DPIA Summary — Automated Decisions.

10. Right of withdrawal

The 14-day right of withdrawal under arts. 52 ff. of the Italian Consumer Code does not apply to passenger transport services (art. 59 para. 1 lett. l). This exclusion is by law and we surface it clearly before you book.

11. Liability limits

{{brand_name}} is liable for its intermediation service. It is not liable for the performance of transport, which is the driver's obligation. Nothing excludes liability for wilful misconduct or gross negligence (art. 1229 Italian Civil Code), for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, nor any liability that cannot be excluded by law. For transport-related damage claims, name the driver as the primary counterparty; {{brand_name}} helps you retrieve contact data for the claim.

12. Law, forum, ODR

Italian law applies. As a consumer, you have the right to the forum of your residence or domicile (art. 33 Italian Consumer Code); clauses restricting it are void. The European Commission ODR platform is always available ({{odr_url}}). Complaints: {{email_legal}}. For disputes with the driver, you can also contact the driver's NCC consortium or the Italian Transport Regulatory Authority (ART).
